About the system

What LifeLink is and why blood groups matter

What is LifeLink?

LifeLink is a Blood Donation Management System. Donors create a profile with their blood group and city. People who need blood send a request with the hospital name, the units needed and how urgent it is.

A complete version would keep this information in a database and match donors with requests automatically. This project is only the front end, made with HTML and CSS for the CSE326 course.

Why donate blood?

  • Blood is needed for surgeries, accidents and childbirth.
  • Patients with diseases like thalassemia need blood again and again.
  • Blood cannot be made in a factory, it only comes from donors.
  • Blood does not stay fresh forever, so new donors are always needed.

How it works

Simple steps for donors and for people who need blood

If you want to donate

  1. Fill in the donor registration form.
  2. Enter your blood group, city and last donation date.
  3. We contact you when a request matches your blood group.
  4. Go to the hospital, donate, and rest as the doctor says.

If you need blood

  1. Fill in the blood request form.
  2. Enter the hospital, city, units needed and urgency.
  3. Check availability and the donor list.
  4. Contact a matching donor or the hospital blood bank.
